    Member benefits

    What BUSA provides to members and their families beyond savings and loans.

    All programmes
    Nrs 1,00,000

    Free accidental insurance

    SACCOS has been providing free accidental insurance service to the share members between the age group of 16-59 years. SACCOS pays the yearly premium of the members to the Insurance company for this service.

    Nrs 30,000

    Member relief program

    It is a program by which SACCOS is providing relief support & sympathy to the nominee or family members in the death of the members’. For Participating in this program members should pay Nrs 1,800 for one time.

    Nrs 5,000

    SLC honouring & scholarship program

    Budol Samudayik has been honouring all the child members who pass the School Level Certificate (SLC) every year. Also SACCOS Provides Cash Scholarship of NRS 5000 each for top 4 members.

    75+ years

    Senior member honouring program

    SACCOS has been honouring the Elder members above 75 years of age on the occasion of international senior citizens day. SACCOS honour them by giving money and different useful goods, feeding them, arranging Bhajans for their recreation etc.

    Nrs 1,000

    Maternal mother visit

    Representatives from the Socio–Cultural sub committee visit the maternal mother & the child within 11 days of the birth of the child. Nrs 1000.00 is provided to the mother (member); in the case of caesarean delivery Nrs. 5000.00 is also provided.

    8 partners

    Money transfer service

    Budol Samudayik SACCOS has been providing money transfer service to the community people being the agent & sub agent of different remittance companies.

      Voice of Chairman and Commitment From CEO

      Budol Samudayik SACCOS is a primary co-operative institution established and operated in the community as per the governing principles and norms of cooperatives. The commencement of this institution was made with the noble objective of collecting scattered capital in the community with the help of regular saving and assisting its members in fulfilling their financial necessities.
      Kailash K.C.President
