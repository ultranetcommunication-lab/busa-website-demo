    More than a place to save

    The cooperative stands with members and their families at the moments that matter: a birth, an exam result, an accident, a loss.

    • Rs 1,00,000accidental death relief

      Free accidental insurance

      Share members aged 16 to 59 are insured at no cost. BUSA pays the yearly premium to the insurance company on every member's behalf.

    • Rs 30,000to the member's family

      Member relief programme

      When a member passes away, representatives of the board and staff visit the family within 12 days with financial relief and sympathy.

    • Rs 5,000scholarship, top 4 students

      SLC honouring and scholarship

      Every child member who passes the School Leaving Certificate is honoured each year. Cash scholarships go to the top boy and girl from both private and community schools.

    • 75+years, honoured since 2061

      Senior member honouring

      Members aged over 75 are honoured on International Senior Citizens Day with cash, useful gifts and a shared meal.

    • 11 daysvisit after a birth

      Maternal mother visit

      The socio-cultural sub-committee visits mother and newborn within 11 days of birth, with Rs 1,000 for the mother.

    • Freecopies and pencils

      School supplies for child members

      Exercise books and pencils distributed to child savers at the start of the school year.

    • 8remittance partners

      Money transfer

      Receive money from family abroad at any service centre.

    From six members to nearly twenty thousand

    BUSA began in former Ward 11 of Banepa with six members saving Rs 100 each, to build a habit of regular saving.

    1. 2050B.S.

      Registered on 2 Kartik at the District Co-operative Office, Kavre. Joins NEFSCUN.

    2. 2057B.S.

      Operations begin in Magh with six members and Rs 100 each.

    3. 2061B.S.

      First senior member honouring programme.

    4. 2064B.S.

      Joins the ACCESS programme run by NEFSCUN and ACCU, the turning point for growth.

    5. 2069B.S.

      ACCESS Bronze brand from ACCU.

    6. 2070B.S.

      National Co-operative Business Award, National Co-operative Federation.

    7. 2072B.S.

      ACCESS Silver brand from ACCU.

    8. 2074B.S.

      NEFSCUN Best SACCOS award.

    9. 2083B.S.

      27th anniversary: 19,908 members, five service centres, Rs 2.36 arba in assets.
